Cookie Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Eventeria uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, provide a better user experience, and give website owners information about how their sites are being used.

2. How Eventeria Uses Local Storage (Not Cookies)

Eventeria itself does not set HTTP cookies on your device. Instead, we use your browser's localStorage API to store small amounts of data locally on your device. This data includes:

  • Theme preference — whether you prefer light or dark mode
  • RSVP / going state — events you have marked as "going" using the checkmark on event pages
  • Filter preferences — your last-used category or city filter selections

This data is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services we use may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies. The services are:

3.1 Google Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google may collect data about font requests, which may include your IP address. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3.2 unpkg.com (Lucide Icons)

We load the Lucide icon library from unpkg.com. This CDN may log standard request data including your IP address.

3.3 Supabase

Our backend is powered by Supabase. When you submit an event or interact with features that connect to our database, requests are made to Supabase's infrastructure. Supabase may set session cookies for authenticated admin users. See Supabase's Privacy Policy for details.

4. Your Choices

You have several options for managing cookies and local storage:

  • Browser settings — Most browsers allow you to block cookies, clear existing cookies, and configure alerts when cookies are set. Consult your browser's help documentation for instructions.
  • Clear site data — You can clear all locally stored data for Eventeria by visiting your browser's developer tools or site settings and clearing storage for this domain.
  • Opt-out of Google — You can opt out of Google's use of cookies for advertising via Google's Ads Settings.

Please note that disabling cookies or local storage may affect the functionality of Eventeria, including the ability to save theme preferences and RSVP state.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Eventeria after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
